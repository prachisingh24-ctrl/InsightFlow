# InsightFlow

InsightFlow turns raw customer feedback — reviews, surveys, support messages, social posts — into clear, prioritized business insights.

**Who it's for:** product, CX and operations teams who need to understand how customers feel, spot recurring problems early, and decide what to fix first.

**What it does:**
- Collects feedback by hand, via CSV import, or from a sample dataset; searchable and filterable.
- AI analyzes each item for sentiment, category, topics, core complaint and urgency.
- Dashboard shows key metrics, sentiment distribution, weekly trends and top complaints.
- Generates ranked issues with impact scores, data-backed reasoning and action items; issues can be tracked through to resolution.
- Produces executive reports that can be shared by link, printed to PDF, or downloaded.

**Access:** visitors land on a public home page and either sign in by email (magic link) or try a live demo. The demo needs no account: it loads sample feedback and runs the whole AI workflow. Signed-in users can "Raise a concern", which writes an email to the InsightFlow support team.

**Adaption:** the AI adapts to each business. Teams add business context and category definitions, and every manual correction to an AI analysis is saved as a learned rule that is fed into all future analyses, recommendations and reports.
